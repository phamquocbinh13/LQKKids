## Brand & Style
This design system establishes a warm, trustworthy, and joyful e-commerce and brand presence tailored for modern Vietnamese families seeking comfortable, high-quality children's fashion. Taking direct cues from joyful tri-color storytelling (sky blue, warm golden yellow, and fresh leaf green), the aesthetic balances whimsical warmth with dependable parental reassurance.

The visual style blends soft organic forms with crisp, commercial polish:
- **Warm Reassurance:** Instead of clinical cool neutrals, the canvas is built upon cozy dairy creams and milk-warm backdrops paired with deep cocoa and roasted chestnut typography, creating a caring embrace.
- **Playful Dynamism:** Pill shapes, rounded badge tags, soft bubbled container cards, and subtle bounce animations celebrate childhood delight without devolving into chaotic clutter.
- **Modern Vietnamese Retail Precision:** Layouts emphasize clarity for busy parents on mobile devices—prominent trust badges (safety, organic cotton, breathable craft), large tactile touch targets, and scannable visual categorization (boy, girl, baby, seasonal).

## Colors
The color palette relies on a harmonious trio of energetic primary hues balanced by soothing pastel tints and rich, earthy neutrals:

- **Primary Sky Blue (`#5B9BD5`):** Denotes clarity, trust, and breathability. Used for primary interactive actions, key category highlights, selected tabs, and loyalty incentives.
- **Secondary Warm Yellow (`#F6C343`):** Evokes golden sunshine, optimism, and warmth. Applied to promotional callouts, star ratings, joyful promotional banners, and badges.
- **Tertiary Fresh Green (`#9BCB65`):** Reflects natural materials, organic cotton certifications, and healthy active play. Utilized for eco-labels, success states, and fresh collection tags.
- **Warm Cream (`#FFF9EF`):** The master canvas tone, serving as a gentle, comforting alternative to stark pure white.
- **Pastel Container Tints:**
  - **Soft Blue (`#EEF6FD`):** Surfaces for boys' and baby collections, informational banners, and active filter chips.
  - **Soft Yellow (`#FFF7D6`):** Highlights for discounts, gift bundles, and seasonal offers.
  - **Soft Green (`#F0F8E8`):** Surfaces for safety guarantees, material origin badges, and eco-friendly packaging callouts.
- **Text & Stroke Structure:**
  - **Main Text (`#49372C`):** Deep warm cocoa providing high contrast and softer readability than pure black.
  - **Secondary Text (`#76685E`):** Warm milk-chocolate taupe for metadata, sizing specs, and breadcrumbs.
  - **Light Border (`#EDE5DA`):** Delicately outlines cards, product dividers, and input borders.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans was selected across all typography levels for its friendly curves, open apertures, and modern warmth. It effortlessly renders Vietnamese diacritics with high clarity and balance. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Set with confident semi-bold and extra-bold weights. The rounded terminal strokes echo the bubbly, friendly letterforms of children's fashion branding while staying sophisticated and premium.
- **Body Text:** Ample line height (1.5x to 1.6x) and balanced letter spacing ensure product specs, fabric certifications, and sizing guides remain effortless to digest on mobile screens under various lighting conditions.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Slightly tracked uppercase on `label-sm` lends clean structure to sale pills, age groups (e.g., `2Y - 4Y`), and safety certifications.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an adaptive 8pt baseline rhythm inside an open, fluid grid system:

- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and outer margins. Touch areas strictly conform to a minimum 48px footprint to facilitate one-handed navigation by parents on the move.
- **Tablet (768px to 1023px):** 8-column grid with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and outer margins. Product carousels transform into 2-to-3 item grids.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of 1280px with 32px (`2rem`) gutters and 56px (`3.5rem`) side margins. Visual balance leans into generous whitespace with breathing room around product imagery.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is soft, pillowy, and tactile—reminiscent of plush cotton garments and clouds:

- **Ambient Soft Shadows:** Surfaces float with light, warm-tinted drop shadows rather than harsh dark grays. The shadow formula uses `rgba(73, 55, 44, 0.06)` with a generous 16px to 24px blur and low vertical displacement (4px to 8px), evoking soft diffused natural sunlight.
- **Tonal Layering:** Product cards, review bubbles, and filter containers sit atop the `#FFF9EF` background using pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pastel tints (`#EEF6FD`, `#FFF7D6`, `#F0F8E8`).
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Interactive components and resting cards feature a gentle 1px border using Light Border (`#EDE5DA`). This retains distinct structural boundaries without creating visual rigidity.
- **Active & Hover Lift:** Interactive cards smoothly elevate by 2px with an enhanced warm shadow (`rgba(73, 55, 44, 0.10)`) to indicate responsiveness.

## Shapes
In harmony with the soft, friendly brand identity, the shape language defaults to level 3 (Pill-shaped):

- **Micro Elements (Buttons, Tags, Badges):** Full pill radius (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for friendly child-safe ergonomics.
- **Mid Elements (Cards, Dialogue Modals, Product Tiles):** Sized with generous radii (`rounded-2xl` at 1.25rem / 20px up to `rounded-3xl` at 1.75rem / 28px), mirroring the curved silhouettes of the logo illustration.
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** Soft pill or rounded-2xl finishes provide a cohesive, tactile feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped, Sky Blue (`#5B9BD5`) background with crisp white typography, 0 8px 18px `rgba(91, 155, 213, 0.25)` drop shadow. Smooth shrink-on-press (0.98 scale) animation.
- **Secondary / Action Button:** Warm Yellow (`#F6C343`) with Main Text (`#49372C`) font, perfect for "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now" conversion points.
- **Tertiary / Eco Button:** Fresh Green (`#9BCB65`) background with white text, ideal for bundle deals or sustainable line promotions.
- **Soft / Outline Variant:** Pill-shaped with Warm Cream or white fill, 1.5px `#EDE5DA` border, and Main Text (`#49372C`) label.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Fully rounded pills. Inactive states feature Soft Blue (`#EEF6FD`) or white fill with `#EDE5DA` stroke.
- Selected state fills with Sky Blue (`#5B9BD5`) or Warm Yellow (`#F6C343`) with contrasting text and an optional micro heart/star icon prefix.

### Product Cards
- Shaped with `rounded-3xl` (24px radius), set on pure white or Soft Blue with a 1px `#EDE5DA` border and ambient warm shadow.
- Feature an image container with subtle inner rounding, floating heart wishlist icon at the top right, and floating pill badges (e.g., "100% Cotton", "-20%") on the top left.
- Price highlights appear in prominent `title-md` using Main Text (`#49372C`) paired with subtle strikethrough original pricing in Secondary Text (`#76685E`).

### Trust & Reassurance Badges
- Dedicated component replicating the brand's 5-pillar values (Safe for baby, Premium fabric, Natural comfort, Growing with baby, Sustainable fashion).
- Built with a circular or soft squircle container in Soft Yellow (`#FFF7D6`) or Soft Green (`#F0F8E8`), housing minimalist line-and-fill gold/green icons accompanied by compact, centered two-line typography.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Sized with a comfortable 48px height, `rounded-full` or `rounded-2xl` contour, Soft Cream background, and `#EDE5DA` boundary.
- Focused state transitions border color smoothly to Sky Blue (`#5B9BD5`) with a 3px soft outer ring in `rgba(91, 155, 213, 0.2)`. Placeholder text in Secondary Text (`#76685E`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Radio options are circular with an inner solid colored dot upon selection.
- Checkboxes use `rounded-lg` (8px) corners with Fresh Green (`#9BCB65`) or Sky Blue (`#5B9BD5`) fill and a clean white checkmark icon.

### Size Selector Chips
- Rounded square buttons (44x44px, `rounded-xl`) indicating baby/child ages or clothing measurements (e.g., "3-6M", "1Y", "2Y"). Unselected states show neutral borders; selected states highlight with Sky Blue border and Soft Blue fill.
